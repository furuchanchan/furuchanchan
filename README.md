## Hi, I'm Kotaro Furuno

Independent engineer and founder at [TechWorker Inc.](https://techworker.co.jp) (Tokyo). I build AI products and keep a foot in open source.

### Building & maintaining

- **[japan-synthetic-personas](https://github.com/furuchanchan/japan-synthetic-personas)** — 3,000 statistically-grounded synthetic Japanese consumer personas (CC BY 4.0). [Dataset on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/furuchanchan/japan-synthetic-personas).
- **AI agent operating layer** — my daily driver runs on [gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain) (persistent agent memory) and [gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) (opinionated build workflow), with local patches and version discipline.
